Fostering an Ecosystem of Excellence

Established with the vision of making PICT a global hub for entrepreneurship, SIC provides end-to-end support for visionary founders.

We bridge the gap between academia and industry. Through rigorous mentorship, strategic funding partnerships, and state-of-the-art infrastructure, we ensure that technological breakthroughs within our campus successfully transition into the global market.

  • Seed Funding & VC Networking
